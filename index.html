Gut & Microbiome Health

Microbiome Analysis · Leaky Gut · IBS · SIBO · Dysbiosis

Your gut houses 70% of your immune system and 95% of your serotonin. When the microbiome is disrupted, the effects ripple through every system — from mood to metabolism to immunity.

🦠 The Gut-Brain-Immune Axis

The gut microbiome communicates with the brain via the vagus nerve, producing over 30 neurotransmitters and directly modulating immune responses. Our 3-phase gut restoration protocol uses specific probiotic strains, prebiotic foods, and intestinal repair nutrients to rebuild a resilient microbiome from the inside out.

Comprehensive Stool Analysis SIBO Breath Test Intestinal Permeability Food Sensitivity Panel Microbiome Mapping
🥦 Cruciferous Vegetables

Glucosinolates feed Akkermansia muciniphila — the bacterium that strengthens gut barrier integrity, reducing leaky gut by up to 40%.

🫙 Fermented Foods

Lactobacillus species in yogurt & kimchi produce lactic acid, lowering gut pH and suppressing harmful pathogens while boosting immune tolerance.

🧄 Prebiotic Fiber

Inulin, FOS, and resistant starch selectively feed beneficial bacteria, increasing SCFA production (butyrate) for colonocyte fuel and reduced inflammation.

🫐 Polyphenol-Rich Berries

Anthocyanins act as prebiotics, shifting microbiome diversity toward anti-inflammatory species with measurable improvements in as little as 4 weeks.

Metabolic Reset Program

Sustainable weight management requires understanding your unique metabolic blueprint. We go beyond calories to address insulin signalling, hormonal balance, and the metabolic root causes of weight gain.

⚡ The Insulin-Fat Storage Loop

Chronically elevated insulin (driven by refined carbohydrates and seed oils) locks fat cells in storage mode, making weight loss nearly impossible despite caloric restriction. Our metabolic reset protocol focuses on insulin sensitization through timed nutrition, specific food compounds, and targeted movement protocols.

🍏 Apple Cider Vinegar

Stress & Wellness Program

HPA Axis · Cortisol Rhythms · Nutritional Psychiatry · Adaptogens

Stress is not just psychological — it's deeply physiological. Chronic HPA axis dysregulation depletes key nutrients, disrupts sleep architecture, impairs digestion, and accelerates biological aging.

🧠 The Cortisol–Nutrition Feedback Loop

Chronic cortisol elevation depletes magnesium (50mg lost per acute stress event), Vitamin C (highest concentration in the body is in the adrenal glands), and B vitamins. This creates a vicious cycle — stress depletes nutrients, which amplifies stress response. Targeted repletion breaks the cycle from the inside out.

Cortisol 4-Point Saliva Test Magnesium RBC GABA / Serotonin Precursors Adaptogenic Protocol Sleep Architecture Analysis
🍫 Dark Chocolate

Theobromine and flavanols increase BDNF and reduce cortisol. Magnesium content supports GABA receptor function for natural calm.

🫖 Ashwagandha

Withanolides modulate the HPA axis, reducing cortisol by 27.9% in randomized clinical trials. A classically used adaptogen with modern validation.

🥜 Pumpkin Seeds

Tryptophan, zinc, and magnesium — the trifecta for serotonin synthesis and its conversion to melatonin for truly restorative sleep.

🫐 Wild Blueberries

Anthocyanins cross the blood-brain barrier, increasing BDNF and protecting hippocampal neurons from cortisol-induced neuronal atrophy.
